### FitLog

`Next.js` `React` `TypeScript` `Tailwind CSS`

A dark, no-nonsense workout logging app. Browse a library of lifts, check exercise details, and lock up to five workouts into today's plan — everything persists locally, so your plan is waiting when you come back.

[Live Demo](https://assignment-06-pied.vercel.app) &middot; [Source Code](https://github.com/umaer-islam/assignment-06)

### Dev Stack Builder

`React` `TypeScript` `Vite`

Interactive tool for exploring and comparing 12 curated technologies, then assembling a personalized development stack for your next project.

[Source Code](https://github.com/umaer-islam/assignment-05)

### UrukCode

`HTML` `JavaScript` `Open Source`

Free QR code and barcode generator: 10+ barcode formats, QR decoding from images, and PNG/SVG export. Runs entirely in the browser — no data ever leaves your device.

[Live Demo](https://umaer-islam.github.io/urukcode/) &middot; [Source Code](https://github.com/umaer-islam/urukcode)
